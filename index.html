About Fatima Tuz Zahra Islamic Academy

Nurturing souls through authentic Islamic education

Our Mission

At Fatima Tuz Zahra Islamic Academy, we are committed to providing accessible, high-quality Islamic education to students worldwide. Our mission is to illuminate hearts with the sacred knowledge of the Quran and Sunnah, fostering spiritual growth and understanding in every student.

Our Vision

We envision a global community of learners who are deeply connected to the teachings of Islam, equipped with authentic knowledge, and inspired to live by the principles of the Quran in their daily lives.

Our Values

  • Excellence: We strive for the highest standards in teaching and learning.
  • Authenticity: Our curriculum is rooted in authentic Islamic sources.
  • Compassion: We create a supportive and nurturing learning environment.
  • Accessibility: Quality Islamic education should be available to everyone.
